Our Journey

Founded over a decade ago, GroEdge began with a simple yet powerful belief: strategies only create value when they're effectively implemented on the shop floor. While many consultancies focused on delivering presentations, we chose a different path.

We started working directly alongside our clients' teams - in manufacturing plants, warehouses, and operational centers - turning theoretical improvements into tangible results. This hands-on approach became our signature, delivering outcomes that exceeded expectations.

"What sets GroEdge apart is our commitment to implementation. We don't just tell you what to do - we work alongside your team to make it happen, ensuring sustainable results that continue long after we're gone."

— Rajesh Pal, Founder & Principal Consultant
